How it works

A calm, clear
process.

No surprises, no jargon. Just a thoughtful sequence that keeps you in the loop from day one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We visit your space, listen to how you live, and agree on scope, budget and timeline.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Design

    Layouts, 3D views, material palettes and a costed proposal — refined with you before anything is built.

    Weeks 2–4
  3. 03

    Build

    Our team coordinates carpentry, electrical, plumbing and finishes with weekly site updates.

    Weeks 5–12
  4. 04

    Style & handover

    We layer furniture, textiles and art, deep-clean the space and hand over the keys — ready to live in.

    Final week
